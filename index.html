How to read this

Seven strata stack from the neurochemical substrate at the bottom to narrative identity at the top. Each framework is a translucent spindle whose width at a stratum is how much of that stratum it claims. Where two spindles cross, their claims overlap.

  • Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom. Click a spindle to open its native geometry, then click a node to inspect it.
  • Esc goes up a level. / focuses search.
  • The manifold view embeds every type with a published or proxy Big Five vector on the Agency, Plasticity and Communion axes. The slider rotates Stability into the third axis.
